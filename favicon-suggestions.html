Favicon Suggestions

I created the current favicon as a small SVG file: a dark academic-blue rounded square with a simplified white T shape and a lighter vertical accent. Below are more TLI options using the same colors, with Gestalt-style play through closure, figure-ground contrast, and aligned initial forms.

Suggestion A: Constructed TLI

Closest to the current favicon, but with clearer hints of T, L, and I through separate geometric parts.

Suggestion B: Figure-Ground Monogram

A compact monogram where the L and I emerge from the negative space and right-side accent.

Suggestion C: Academic Initial Mark

A calmer academic mark. The dot gives the I a clearer identity while staying restrained.

Suggestion D: Closure Lines

The initials are implied by aligned strokes. It is more abstract and more design-forward.

Suggestion E: Strong T, Hidden LI

Optimized for tiny browser tabs: the T dominates, while L and I are secondary accents.

Suggestion F: Balanced Baseline

A stable, academic-feeling mark with a warm baseline that subtly suggests the L.
